RateBite

Privacy Policy

We don't collect data about you.

Last updated 11 September 2026

The short version

  • RateBite has no accounts and no sign-in — we never ask for your name, email, phone, or location.
  • We don't use analytics or advertising, and we never sell data.
  • Your app preferences stay on your device. The ratings you see are about restaurants, not you.

RateBite is a restaurant-discovery app that blends ratings from multiple sources into one honest score, starting with Bengaluru and Hyderabad. This policy explains, plainly, what happens with data when you use it. It applies to the RateBite mobile app and website.

01 Information we don't collect

RateBite works without an account. There's no sign-up and no login, so we don't ask for or store personal information about you — no name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or payment details. Because we don't build a profile of you, there is no personal profile to lose, share, or sell.

02 Data stored on your device

To make the app pleasant to use, RateBite remembers small preferences locally — for example your most recent search or which view you last opened. This information is stored only in your device's app storage. It stays on your device, is never transmitted to us, and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

03 Information our servers receive

When the app loads restaurant listings and scores, your device makes an ordinary network request to our hosting provider. As with any request on the internet, that provider automatically processes basic technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver a response. We use this only to serve content and keep the service running — not to identify you, track you across apps, or build an advertising profile. We don't combine it with any personal information, because we don't hold any.

04 Where the ratings come from

The heart of RateBite is a single "trust score" computed from restaurant ratings published on public platforms — currently Google and Swiggy — corrected for each platform's bias and review volume. This is factual information about restaurants, not about you. Each restaurant's page shows which sources contributed, so the number is transparent. Source names and links belong to their respective owners.

05 No tracking, ads, or data sales

RateBite contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no social-media trackers. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with anyone, because we don't collect it. If this ever changes, we will update this policy and describe exactly what changed before it takes effect.

06 Service providers

We rely on a small number of providers to run the app:

  • Supabase — hosts the restaurant database the app reads from (see the Supabase privacy policy).
  • App stores — Apple's App Store and Google Play distribute the app and provide us only with aggregate, anonymized install and crash statistics under their own terms.

Restaurant data originates from public platforms including Google and Swiggy, whose policies govern their own services.

07 Children

RateBite is a general-audience app for discovering places to eat and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

08 Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal information tied to you, there is no personal account to access, correct, export, or delete. You remain in full control of the on-device preferences described above: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to remove them at any time. If you have questions about your rights under laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, contact us below.

09 Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how data is handled — for example optional accounts or saved favourites — we will revise this policy, update the date at the top, and, where the change is significant, surface a notice in the app before the new behaviour begins.

10 Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email dutcho.app@gmail.com and we'll respond.

RateBite — one honest rating for where you eat. This policy covers the RateBite app and website. It does not cover the third-party platforms whose ratings we aggregate.
