Croon — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Croon ("Birthday Song With Name: Croon") is made by Shivansh Tekriwal (Dutcho). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Everything stays on your phone

Croon records your voice with the microphone only while you are singing a song. Pitch detection, tuning, mixing and video creation all happen on your device. Your recordings, the names you type and the photos you choose are stored only in the app's temporary storage on your device and are never uploaded to us or anyone else.

When you tap Share, the video is handed to the app you pick (for example Messages or WhatsApp). What happens next is governed by that app.

What we collect

Nothing. Croon has no accounts, no servers, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share data.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The app stores a record of what you bought on your device so it can unlock features, and asks the App Store to restore purchases when you request it.

Permissions

Microphone: to record your singing. Photos: only the photo you pick for the card (through the system photo picker).

Children

Croon does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
